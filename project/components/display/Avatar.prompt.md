Used in customer-proof rows and team listings.

```jsx
<Avatar src="/people/jane.jpg" alt="Jane Cho" />
<Avatar initials="JC" fill="violet" />
```

Default fill is `neutral` (light-gray, `--surface-card`). Pastel fills (`orange`/`pink`/`violet`/`emerald`) are a deliberate, occasional flourish — don't apply them to every avatar on a page.
